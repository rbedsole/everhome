# Diamonds, Enchanting, and the Nether

A major progression chapter closed today.

After branch mining became monotonous, exploration shifted to caving. An expedition produced 18 diamonds, enough to finish the first full diamond equipment set while deliberately leaving the shovel as iron.

The 15-bookshelf enchanting setup was completed after gathering the remaining leather and using 12 books recovered from a village. The first diamond equipment set was then enchanted:

- Pickaxe: Efficiency IV, Unbreaking III
- Axe: Efficiency IV
- Sword: Sharpness IV, Knockback II
- Helmet: Protection III, Aqua Affinity, Unbreaking III
- Chestplate: Protection IV, Unbreaking III, Thorns II
- Leggings: Protection IV, Unbreaking III
- Boots: Protection IV, Unbreaking III

With the enchanting milestone complete, attention turned to the Nether.

The first portal was built at the starter base. On the Nether side, the portal emerged in a Crimson Forest cavern high above a lava sea. A cobbled deepslate room was built around the portal and the location was waypointed.

There is one complication: the portal area is isolated. There is no natural walkable exit from the spawn without mining or building.

## Next Objective

Create a safe route out of the portal cavern and begin the first proper Nether reconnaissance expedition.
