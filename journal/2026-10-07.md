## The platform becomes a settlement canvas

The broad grass platform around Everhome is no longer a rough terraforming job waiting to be fixed. Its perimeter has been manually terraced into the surrounding terrain, leaving a large irregular development area around the 41 × 41 residence.

Four rough edge-center measurements now give the future settlement a useful coordinate framework:

- North: **14132, -10126**
- East: **14206, -10023**
- South: **14149, -9908**
- West: **14083, -10023**

The residence itself centers around **14171, -9943**, placing it toward the southeastern side of the overall development area. Most of the open settlement canvas therefore extends north and west.

The exact village plan is still being designed. The plan is to use MineColonies to turn the surrounding settlement into an inhabited production and logistics network, unless a better compatible alternative emerges before installation. This supersedes the earlier vanilla-villager settlement plan.

## A problem after dark

The size of the blank grass platform became obvious at night for a less charming reason: hostile mobs could spawn across a huge amount of open ground.

The planned answer is Everhome's first **Diamond Magnum Torch**. Its current configuration suppresses monster spawning in a broad ellipsoid around the torch, but its recipe introduces one important bottleneck: a **Fire Charge**.

Everhome has the diamonds. What it does not yet have is Blaze Powder.

That turns the next Nether trip from general exploration into a mission.

## Everhome's portals

Two adjacent Nether portals now stand near the residence. Neither has yet been entered from Everhome.

Their Overworld positions calculate to essentially the same Nether destination, approximately **1771, -1243**. The exact portal position and Y level remain unknown until the first crossing.

Pre-expedition map reconnaissance places the predicted arrival area inside a **Crimson Forest**.

## Reconnaissance before the crossing

Using Everhome's world seed and Java 26.2 terrain data, several nearby Nether structures were mapped before the expedition. These are **predicted targets, not in-game discoveries**.

The primary fortress target is at **1931, -1365**, roughly 201 blocks horizontally from the calculated portal position. Two alternatives were also recorded at **2011, -1109** and **1451, -1141**.

A **Hoglin Stables bastion** is mapped farther northwest at **1438, 33, -1480**. It is being saved for a later expedition rather than folded into the first trip.

## Expedition 01

The plan is deliberately narrow: cross from Everhome for the first time, record and waypoint the actual Nether portal, secure the arrival area if necessary, then assess the terrain toward the primary fortress.

If that route is practical, the objective is to reach the fortress and return with Blaze Rods. Nether Wart and soul sand are worthwhile secondary prizes, but survival and the first Blaze Rod come first.

One Blaze Rod is enough to break the immediate bottleneck. A safer haul of several rods would establish a useful reserve.

Back at Everhome, Blaze Powder can become a Fire Charge, and the Fire Charge can complete the Diamond Magnum Torch.

The Nether is no longer an unknown direction from the permanent home. It has become a mapped frontier waiting for the first crossing.
