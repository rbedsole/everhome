## Latest Milestone

The first Nether portal is active. The Nether-side portal is protected by a cobbled deepslate room, but the portal spawned isolated in a huge Crimson Forest cavern above a lava sea. There is no natural walkable route out, so the next expedition begins with tunneling or building a safe route to explorable terrain.
