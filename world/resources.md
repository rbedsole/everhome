# Resources

Everhome's practical guide to obtaining the things the world actually needs.

Resources focuses on **sources, bottlenecks, renewable supplies, and useful infrastructure**, not a constantly changing count of every item in storage. Exact inventory data belongs in live world tooling when it is available.

## Current Resource Picture

Everhome does not currently have a major resource bottleneck.

The early leather shortage that slowed the enchanting setup was resolved, and the first full level-30 enchanting setup is complete. Diamond equipment is established, while large reserves of building material from deep mining remain available.

---

## Resource Sources

### Building Stone

Deep mining has produced a substantial supply of cobbled deepslate, making it one of Everhome's readily available durable building materials.

The mining station is the primary source and storage point for material produced by the branch mine.

### Diamonds

Early diamonds came from the Y -58 branch mine. When branch mining became repetitive, caving proved a more enjoyable alternative and produced the diamonds needed to finish the first diamond equipment set.

There is currently no reason to treat diamond collection as a mandatory grind.

### Leather

Leather was the main bottleneck while building the level-30 enchanting setup.

The starter base's cow herd provides a renewable source. Books recovered from a village reduced the amount of leather needed for the original bookshelf build, and the immediate shortage is now resolved.

### Sugar Cane & Paper

Sugar cane is established and was not the limiting factor during the enchanting push. It provides a renewable source of paper for books and other future uses.

### Food

Livestock at the starter base provides a renewable food source. Food production can be expanded or automated later if normal play creates a reason to do so.

---

## Farms & Renewable Resources

Everhome is open to farms and automation when they are useful, interesting, or remove an actual recurring annoyance.

A farm does not need to exist simply because one can be built. When a farm becomes important enough to the world, its resource output can be documented here while the build itself can be represented as a World Operations project if it is created there.

No major automated resource farms have been documented yet.

---

## Resource History

Resource shortages can be part of Everhome's story without remaining permanent warnings.

For example, leather was once a progression bottleneck because the enchanting room required bookshelves. Once enough leather and village books were gathered, that bottleneck ended. The journal preserves that history while this page reflects the current situation.

## Future Live Data

If the Everhome website is eventually combined with World Operations remote access, this section could gain a live inventory/search layer backed by actual world data.

That would allow the website to answer questions such as **“How much iron do I have?”** or **“Where is my cobbled deepslate stored?”** without turning this manually maintained page into an inventory database.

Until then, Resources remains the durable knowledge layer: where materials come from, what is renewable, what has been scarce, and what infrastructure supports them.
