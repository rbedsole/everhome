## Major Milestones

<div class="milestone-list">
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>Permanent Home Chosen</strong><p>The former Candidate 1 mountain-and-plains site is now simply Everhome: the permanent center of the world.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>Everhome Started</strong><p>Everhome's main residence has been placed at the foot of the mountain. The complete initial build occupies a 41 × 41 footprint.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>Settlement Ground Established</strong><p>The large development platform around the residence has been expanded and manually terraced into the surrounding terrain, creating room for a long-term settlement rather than only the house footprint.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>Everhome Portals Built</strong><p>Two adjacent Overworld Nether portals have been constructed near the residence. They have not yet been entered from Everhome, so the permanent Nether-side arrival point remains to be confirmed in-game.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>First Nether Access</strong><p>The world's first Nether portal was established and secured during the starter-base era. That portal is now legacy infrastructure rather than part of the permanent transport network.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>First Enchanted Diamond Set</strong><p>Diamond combat gear and primary tools were completed and enchanted during the starter-base era.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>Level-30 Enchanting</strong><p>A full 15-bookshelf enchanting setup was established at the starter base.</p></div></div>
  <div class="milestone"><span class="milestone-mark">✓</span><div><strong>Deep Mining Established</strong><p>A dedicated Y -58 mining station and branch mine supported early progression.</p></div></div>
</div>

## Immediate Objective

<div class="ever-callout"><strong>Blaze Rods for spawn control.</strong><p>The blank settlement platform exposed a large hostile-mob spawning problem. Everhome's first Diamond Magnum Torch requires a Fire Charge, making Blaze Powder the current bottleneck. The first Everhome Nether expedition therefore has a concrete mission: secure the portal, reach a fortress, obtain Blaze Rods, and return safely. Nether Wart is a valuable secondary prize if available.</p></div>

## Development Roadmap

<div class="frontier-grid">
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>1 · Settle Everhome</strong><span>Finish making the permanent home operational: complete the first Nether expedition, establish spawn suppression for the developed settlement area, and organize supplies for the next major build.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>2 · Mountain Storage</strong><span>The storage build is already chosen. Excavate for it with WorldEdit, construct it in the mountain, and integrate automatic sorting and useful storage/logistics mods.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>3 · Renewable Production</strong><span>Add crop, livestock, wood, and other production buildings as actual needs arise. Paths grow organically between useful destinations.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>4 · Everhome Settlement</strong><span>Develop a living settlement around the residence. MineColonies is under active consideration as the population, building, production, and internal logistics framework; the earlier vanilla-villager plan remains an alternative until that decision is made in-world.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>5 · Industrial Resources</strong><span>Build a permanent quarry/mining facility while Simple Quarry heads move to fresh extraction areas as old sites reach the bottom of the world.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>6 · Nether Network</strong><span>Everhome's portal becomes kilometre zero for a new long-distance network. The abandoned starter-base portal does not need to be connected.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>7 · Mature Infrastructure</strong><span>Add logistics, mob protection, specialized gear, transport, remote storage access, and other conveniences when the world creates a reason for them.</span></div>
</div>

<div class="ever-note"><strong>Working rhythm:</strong> keep one major project and one side project active rather than treating the roadmap as a rigid checklist. Everhome should grow from actual needs and discoveries.</div>
