## Current Direction

No new major gameplay project has been chosen. Finish MineColonies layout before colony construction. Existing base will not be renovated. Standalone renewable-resource farms are possibilities, not commitments.

## Historical Development Roadmap

<div class="frontier-grid">
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>1 · Settle Everhome</strong><span>Finish making the permanent home operational: complete the first Nether expedition, establish spawn suppression for the developed settlement area, and organize supplies for the next major build.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>2 · Mountain Storage</strong><span>The storage build is already chosen. Excavate for it with WorldEdit, construct it in the mountain, and integrate automatic sorting and useful storage/logistics mods.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>3 · Renewable Production</strong><span>Add crop, livestock, wood, and other production buildings as actual needs arise. Paths grow organically between useful destinations.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>4 · Everhome Settlement</strong><span>Develop a living settlement around the residence. MineColonies is the planned population, building, production, and internal logistics framework unless a demonstrably better compatible alternative is found before installation. The colony should grow around the existing player residence rather than replace it.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>5 · Industrial Resources</strong><span>Build a permanent quarry/mining facility while Simple Quarry heads move to fresh extraction areas as old sites reach the bottom of the world.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>6 · Nether Network</strong><span>Everhome's portal becomes kilometre zero for a new long-distance network. The abandoned starter-base portal does not need to be connected.</span></div>
  <div class="frontier-card"><strong>7 · Mature Infrastructure</strong><span>Add logistics, mob protection, specialized gear, transport, remote storage access, and other conveniences when the world creates a reason for them.</span></div>
</div>

<div class="ever-note"><strong>Working rhythm:</strong> keep one major project and one side project active rather than treating the roadmap as a rigid checklist. Everhome should grow from actual needs and discoveries.</div>


> **Historical note:** The roadmap above preserves older ideas, including mountain storage and Nether networks, but some are superseded or not currently useful. For current status consult the [State of the World]({{ '/docs/state-of-the-world' | relative_url }}).
