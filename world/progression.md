# Progression

## Completed Milestones

- Established the starter base
- Built animal pens and began livestock breeding
- Reached diamond mining depth and established a branch-mining station
- Crafted the first diamond pickaxe
- Completed the first full diamond combat/tool set, excluding a diamond shovel by choice
- Built a full 15-bookshelf level-30 enchanting setup
- Enchanted the first diamond equipment set
- Established Nether access
- Secured and waypointed the Nether-side portal

## Current Chapter

### Into the Nether

The immediate goal is to escape the isolated portal cavern safely and begin exploring the surrounding Nether.

## Longer-Term Direction

Everhome's current base is temporary. Finding and developing the permanent home site remains a major future chapter. Equipment upgrades such as Fortune III, Feather Falling IV, stronger helmet enchantments, and eventually Mending can happen alongside exploration rather than blocking progression.
