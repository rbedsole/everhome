# Locations

## Starter Base

The current operational home of Everhome. It is intentionally a starter base rather than the world's permanent centerpiece.

## Mining Station

Located at Y -58. The station supports the branch mine with storage, crafting, and smelting infrastructure.

The main corridor begins around X -1037, Z -497 and runs west.

## Nether Portal

The first Nether connection. The Nether-side portal is enclosed in cobbled deepslate and waypointed.

The portal spawned in an isolated Crimson Forest cavern above a large lava sea. There is currently no natural walkable route from the portal area to the surrounding terrain.
