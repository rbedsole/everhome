# Projects

The things Everhome is deliberately building, changing, or working toward.

Not every task is a project. A quick resource run, ordinary exploration, or replacing a worn tool does not need a project card. Projects are reserved for work substantial enough to have a goal, leave a mark on the world, or unfold across multiple sessions.

## What Counts as a Project?

Everhome's project list should stay useful rather than becoming a giant task manager.

A project generally belongs here when it involves a meaningful build, piece of infrastructure, world change, long-term goal, or multi-session effort. Smaller objectives can live in the journal, progression pages, or simply remain part of normal play.

Projects can be added, paused, reshaped, or abandoned as the world changes. Completion is not the only interesting outcome.
