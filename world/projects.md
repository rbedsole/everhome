# Projects

Everhome projects are the projects created and managed through the **World Operations** mod.

World Operations is the source of truth for whether something is a project. This page documents those projects as part of Everhome's history and provides a world-journal view of work that is being managed in-game.

## Active Projects

No World Operations projects have been documented here yet.

When a project is created in World Operations, it can be represented here with information such as its name, status, connected site, purpose, progress, and relevant journal entries.

## Completed Projects

No completed World Operations projects have been documented yet.

Completed projects can remain here as part of Everhome's permanent history rather than disappearing when the work is finished.

---

## World Operations Integration

A project belongs on this page when it exists as a project in World Operations.

Ordinary objectives, exploration, equipment upgrades, resource runs, and other gameplay activities do **not** automatically become projects. They can still appear in the World Journal or Progression sections without being promoted to project status.

Where appropriate, a project can connect several parts of the Everhome record:

- **World Operations** manages the project in-game.
- **Sites and locations** establish where the work belongs.
- **The World Journal** records what happened while working on it.
- **Projects** preserves the project's overall history and current state.

As World Operations develops, this page can evolve alongside its project system rather than inventing a second, competing definition of projects.
